### 1.2 The gate components (proposal §10)

**CH4.4, kD sweep with the FMM3D gate.** §10 asks for the FMM against the direct sum on W1 to
kD = 1000, W2 to 500 and W3 to 200, beside `hfmm3d`; the full-target crosscheck mapped to
levels and classes, no error concentrated at a class boundary; time, memory and build per k.

- Accuracy (CH4.4a §2): every case within ε at 10⁻³, 10⁻⁶ and 10⁻⁸ (largest 0.76 ε, ∇φ, W2
  with the constant density at kD = 100, 10⁻⁶). "W3 to 200" read as §9.1 states W3: resolved to
  kD = 100 (4.0·10⁶ points) and under-resolved at N = 10⁶ for kD = 200 and 500 (signed off on
  #255, question 1).
- Crosscheck (CH4.4a §3, CH4.4b): against FMM3D at 10⁻¹² (`fastker` at kD = 1000, where
  `default` exceeds the node). All-target errors within ε; worst box ≤ 68.2 ε within D1; 114.7 ε
  on W1 at kD = 1000, the plane wave's trace, 10⁻⁸, the truncation of levels 2–5 at a tangent
  point (every level +1 degree: 48.4 ε for 2–4 % more evaluation). §9.1 gates 100 ε within D1
  and reports beyond it (amended at CH4.4b, #256 question 2).
- No class-boundary concentration (CH4.4b §3–§4): the form on RCR 1.000 in every worst box
  (154 runs), the classes beside the bound's threshold on RCR the same (55), Batched on RCR the
  same (11); where the waves run the default errs at most 1.023× RCR at its degree in the worst
  box, 1.034× in ℓ2, read one-sided (§9.1 as amended).
- Time, memory, build per k (CH4.4a §4–§7): W1's build 0.09–0.13 s at kD = 100, 2.6–2.9 s at
  500, 16–19 s at 1000 (the RCR tables of level 2 9.2–12.9 s of it); our resident growth 21–29 GB
  on W1 at kD = 1000; FMM3D `fastker` 45–118 GB there, `default` beyond the node's 180 GB.

**CH4.5, host performance at matched accuracy.** §10: for each of our tolerances the eps of
FMM3D with the closest error; time and memory, setup apart, one node, on CH4.4's sweep.

- The table of the summary above (CH4.5 §0, §3.1): best split over FMM3D's faster variant at
  the closest eps 0.41/0.31/0.34 (Kathleen, 10⁻³/10⁻⁶/10⁻⁸) and 0.25/0.26/0.26 (locust) over
  26 cases; for N ≥ 10⁶ 0.13–0.18 and 0.13–0.16. The one-sided match gives the same picture.
  "Faster variant" holds on 17 of the 26 cases: on the plane wave at kD = 500 and the eight

  26 about 1.2–1.3× too low on that account, over N ≥ 10⁶ about 1.05× (B11); locust's variants
  are level. The N ≥ 10⁶ figures are the ones to quote.

- Memory (§6): FMM3D's peak over our FMM's growth 2.5× (Kathleen) and 2.2× (locust), geometric

## 3. Measured against the proposal

### 3.1 H4's gain (§5.4, §7; CH4.0e §3; the H1 review's A5)

CH4.0e bounded H4 inside D1 at 14–31 % of build plus one evaluation on W1 at kD = 500 (36–61 %
of the evaluation), and at kD = 1000 at 27–42 %. The review's A5 put the product φ step's gain
at about 2.3× per high-frequency level. Measured:

- A diagonal pair costs 0.08–0.52× an RCR pair at every p (CH4.3b1); the conversions 24–49 % of
  the diagonal levels' thread time (CH4.2b2).
- Under the default against the previous defaults, the evaluation is 0.37× on W1 at kD = 1000
  and 0.47–0.74× at kD = 500, 0.64–0.72× on W3 at kD = 500, 0.86–0.99× at 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁸ on W1 at
  kD ≤ 200 (CH4.3b2c §4): inside the bound, as A5 expected.
- With H4's other tasks: W1 at kD = 500 on Kathleen at the best split went from 9.9, 11.7 and
  19.9 s (CH4.0e, at 10⁻³, 10⁻⁶, 10⁻⁸) to 3.5, 4.5 and 7.5 s (CH4.5), 2.6–2.8× faster, against
  `fastker` 31.6–48.0 s. CH4.0e's Kathleen geometric means over FMM3D's faster variant at matched
