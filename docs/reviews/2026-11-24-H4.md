earlier ones: `docs/reviews/2026-11-12-H6.md`, `docs/reviews/2026-11-19-H1.md`). There were two
independent read-only passes over `main` at 4d28d41 (#257, CH4.5). Each was run by one subagent on
Fable (claude-fable-5-2) at medium effort, from the briefs
`.claude/skills/milestone-gate/review-record.md` and `review-architecture.md`. Nothing was
compiled, run or changed. The crates were renamed tetraleaf on 2026-11-21 (#248); this review
uses the new names, the earlier ones the old.

- **Pass 1, the record:** the mathematics, the algorithmic decisions and the benchmarks. It
  started with H4's reports and the Laplace design's mathematics (`laplace-fmm-plan.md` §2, §4),
  then what changed since 8475009.
- **Pass 2, the architecture:** the crate split, the kernel families, the API, bloat, the tests'
  structure and the build. It started with what the H1 review had not reached (tetraleaf-kernels,
  the plan's bodies, the device path), then the changes since 8475009.

The driving session checked the findings marked ✔ against the tree or the document they name:
every finding of high or medium-high severity, and every finding on which the triage rests a
correction or a new task. The others stand as the passes reported them. *Suspicion* marks what a
pass could not verify. New findings continue the earlier letters and numbers. Cite one as

### 1.2 Mathematics

  density.** Proposal §9.1 (amended at CH4.4b); `helmholtz-ch4-4b.md` §5; `helmholtz-ch4-4a.md`

  kD ≤ 100 only. There, at 10⁻⁸, its worst box in ∇φ is 34.9 ε on W1 and 37.6 ε on W2, against

  charges' worst box grows 8.7 → 21.6 → 40.4 ε from kD = 100 to 250 to 500; CH4.3c3 ran the

  same ratio at kD = 500 would put the constant density above 100 ε inside D1. The rule leaves the
  worst pair at 3.3–5.5 ε on every level (CH4.4b), so the pointwise error grows with the number
  of levels by design. *Proposed:* before the gate quotes 68.2 ε, `helmholtz_boxes
  --refine-faces` on W1 with the constant density at kD = 250 and 500 (10⁻³, 10⁻⁶, 10⁻⁸); above
  100 ε, CH4.4b's measured alternative (every high-frequency level one degree up at 10⁻⁸: 114.8 →
  48.4 ε for 2–4 % more evaluation).

### 1.5 Risks

1. The pointwise error beyond D1 and for coherent densities (M15): each doubling of kD adds a
   level.
2. One rank per core: the scratch budget sends coarse levels back to RCR (W1 at kD = 1000: 53 s
   at 40 × 1 against 14.3 s at 1 × 40), the waves cost 8× on W3's dense level, every rank builds
   every diagonal table (about 10 s); CH4.3f, CH4.0k and CH8.0 are off the list, and H8 runs on
   this.
3. The build: 40–67 % of build plus one evaluation at kD = 1000, the RCR tables at p ≈ 245 9–13 s
   of it; the shared serial steps 32–91 % of the build at 1 × N.
4. Complex k and Yukawa (H5, H2): the bound's damping factor over-predicts by 10¹⁴–10²⁵, one
   class at 2.9 E below k a 2.8, the form untested for Yukawa, M13's limits open.
5. E's floor grows about 0.6 u per degree and reaches C₀ u near p ≈ 350, kD ≈ 1400 (CH4.3c1).
6. f32 (H7): M9, and A7 at c_leaf 4 (M17).
7. The machine-class constants (A10) and Kathleen's drift (B12) under every later timing
   decision.

### 2.2 Judged sound, to be left alone

- **The rules hold.** math, ref, tables, simd and kernels have no MPI, octree or RLST; CubeCL is
  in tetraleaf-kernels' manifest alone; `unsafe` only in tetraleaf-simd's `arch` and dispatch,
  tetraleaf-kernels (32 of 32 blocks with `SAFETY:`) and the one allowed example.
- **The rename is clean in code:** no old crate name in any `.rs` or `.toml` of the crates,
  `spikes/`, `tools/` or `.github/`.
- **tetraleaf-plan is still kernel-agnostic after H4:** the empty-box rule (`empty.rs`), the V
  pairs of `near.rs` and `Plan::with_rules_on` take counts and predicates, not wavenumbers.
- **The Helmholtz M2L is chosen in one place per level call** (`helmholtz/operator.rs:3012-3041`:
  waves, the diagonal form, Batched, RCR by pairs); `wave_levels` (CHA.11) is shared.
- **The diagonal form's layering:** ref defines it, tables stores it (`helmholtz/diagonal/`), exec
  calls it per level (`operator_diagonal.rs`), each tested against the layer below; the level
  call allocates only on first growth (`operator_diagonal.rs:880-883`).
- **`tetraleaf_kernels::plane_wave`:** one kernel body for both layouts through `#[comptime]`
  (`plane_wave.rs:1189-1221`), the form the older kernels should have (L9).
- **Markers:** no TODO, FIXME, `todo!`, `unimplemented!` or `#[deprecated]`; every `allow` has
  its reason.
- **T6. H4's fast paths have slower trusted paths in the right crates:** the tables' diagonal
  form against ref, the operator against RCR (`tests/helmholtz/diagonal.rs`, `m2l_override.rs`),
  the cost model by hand in unit tests.
