# Project review at the H4 gate, second run (2026-11-25)

This is the fourth project review. A review is repeated at every milestone gate (`/milestone-gate`,
step 3). The earlier reviews are `docs/reviews/2026-11-12-H6.md`, `docs/reviews/2026-11-19-H1.md`
and `docs/reviews/2026-11-24-H4.md`. This one was made for the H4 gate's second run.

The first run, on 2026-11-24 at 4d28d41, kept H4 open for CH4.8 (`docs/helmholtz/gates/H4.md` §9).
This run read `main` at 03bc2bd (#261, CH4.8b). It made two independent read-only passes, each by
one subagent on Fable (claude-fable-5-2) at medium effort, from the briefs
`.claude/skills/milestone-gate/review-record.md` and `review-architecture.md`. Nothing was compiled,
run or changed.

The only product change since the last review is CH4.8b's `DegreeRule::margin`. Both passes
therefore spent most of their reading on what the last review had not reached.

- **Pass 1, the record:** the mathematics, the algorithmic decisions and the benchmarks. It began
  with CH4.8a, CH4.8b and the first gate run, then read `optimisation.md`, `scale-out.md` and the
  Phase 5S report (the last review's "not read").
- **Pass 2, the architecture:** it read the diff since 4d28d41 in full. It then read
  tetraleaf-octree's bodies, `tune.rs`, the device examples' overlap, the test bodies of
  `helmholtz_fmm.rs` and the workflows other than `run-tests.yml`.

The driving session checked the findings marked ✔ against the tree or the document they name. It
checked every finding on which the triage rests a correction, a new task or the gate's run on its
branch. The others stand as the passes reported them. *Suspicion* marks what a pass could not
verify. New findings continue the earlier letters and numbers; cite one as
"review 2026-11-25, M19".

### 1.2 Mathematics

- **M18 (medium) ✔, partly answered by the gate run. The margin leaves the plateau alone, and
  CH4.8b's largest remaining error is the plateau's.**
  - *Where:* `helmholtz-ch4-8b.md` §1; `helmholtz-ch4-8a.md` §5–§8.
  - *Checked:* the largest ℓ2 under the default is W2 at kD = 100, 10⁻⁶, at degrees 35, 22, 15×3,
    and the margin moved it only from 0.76 ε to 0.67 ε. CH4.8a found the plateau carries about a
    third of ∇φ's Σ|e|² at 10⁻⁶ under `h1`.
  - *The concern:* coherent stacking over levels also applies to plateau levels. No workload of the
    verification has more than three plateau levels with a far field, and W4 never ran with the
    constant density.
  - *Suspicion:* a locally refined surface, or 30–40 points per wavelength throughout, adds plateau
    levels and may pass ε at 10⁻⁶.
  - *Proposed by the pass:* the constant density on W4's four distributions at kD = 10 and 100, and

### 2.2 Judged sound, to be left alone

- **The rules hold.**
  - math, ref, tables, simd and kernels have no octree, plan, exec or `mpi::` in their src.
  - CubeCL is a dependency of tetraleaf-kernels alone.
  - `unsafe` appears only where the root CLAUDE.md allows it.
- **The host kernel abstraction:** both families implement the crate-private `HostKernel<T>`
  (`driver.rs:63`). The level drivers are generic over it and statically dispatched. The
  near-copies are in the facades (L1), not here.
- **CH4.8b's change has the right shape:** a field on `DegreeRule`, the default written as
  `Self { margin: 1, ..Self::CH4_3C3 }` (`degree.rs:295-298`), with no new code path.
- **`tune.rs`** (2,141 lines, read in outline plus `decide`) makes one decision loop in the order
  cached, static, timed. Its cache codec has a round-trip test. Its `MAGIC = "NDFMMTUN"` is a file
  format and rightly survived the rename.
- **tetraleaf-octree's distributed machinery is private** (`pub(crate)`). Its pure helpers are
  tested against brute force.
- **`helmholtz_fmm.rs`'s test bodies** check each scenario against the direct sum, threads against
  serial, pairs against level calls, and P ranks against one rank over the union, bit for bit. Only
  the file's size is an issue (T2).
- **The device kernel pairs (L9, now read) are not near-copies.** `expand_cube_kernel` against
  `expand_cpu_kernel` differ in 155 of 313 lines; the shared arithmetic is already in helper
  `#[cube]` functions.
