# tetraleaf-math

Purpose: Legendre functions, real solid harmonics and gradients, rotation blocks,
index layout and the scalar trait that every other tetraleaf-* crate builds on.
Phase and components: Phase 0, C0.1–C0.3 (tasks T2, T4, T5 in docs/phase0/).
Helmholtz CH1.2a added `orthonormal` (CONVENTIONS §3.15): the normalised Legendre
functions of spike S1 (telescoped near the poles, an exponent per column), the real
orthonormal harmonics with ∂θ and (1/sin θ) ∂φ, and the rotation blocks at any degree
(Gumerov–Duraiswami y-blocks, the π − β signs, z-y-z blocks); its tests are
`tests/orthonormal/` (degrees above 300 ignored, run in release).
Helmholtz CH1.2b added `radial` (CONVENTIONS §3.15): the scaled jₙ, hₙ (complex z), iₙ,
kₙ (real x) and their derivatives of spike S3, and re-exports `num_complex::Complex` as
the complex type of the Helmholtz arithmetic; its tests are `tests/radial/`.
Helmholtz CH1.2c added `coaxial` (CONVENTIONS §3.15): the coaxial coefficients
(R|R) = (S|S) and (S|R) of spike S2 in a halved table read in both orientations, by the
recursions and by three projections, chosen by §3.15's rule (the recursion where
Re(k) a ≤ 40); its tests are `tests/coaxial/` (tables beyond p = 100 ignored, run in
release).
Helmholtz CH4.9a2a added `orthonormal::leaf` (`LeafHarmonics`, `LeafScratch`: the orthonormal
harmonics of a batch of points as lanes by the plain three-term recurrence from coefficients
tabulated once, for the leaf operators; within 10 (n + 1)² u √((2n + 1)/(4π)) per term of
`harmonics` in the tests, 1.02 measured, under-range columns zeroed) and `radial::bessel_j_real`
(`bessel_j` at real arguments in real arithmetic, lanes, bit for bit, in f32 but for the
under-range values beyond the turning point, which it zeroes); tests
`tests/orthonormal/leaf.rs` and `tests/radial/real.rs`.
Helmholtz CH4.9b1 added the derivatives for L2P's gradient: `LeafHarmonics::{derivative_columns,
derivatives}` (per order Q̄ₙᵐ, `columns`' bits, ∂θ Q̄ₙᵐ and Uₙᵐ = Q̄ₙᵐ / sin θ by a second column of
the same recurrence, no division by sin θ; `harmonics_derivatives`' outputs within
10 (n + 1)³ u √((2n + 1)/(4π)) per term in the tests, 0.87 measured) and
`radial::bessel_j_real_derivatives` (`bessel_j`'s derivatives at real arguments, bit for bit in
f64; `bessel_j_real` keeps its code path and bits). `LeafHarmonics`' directions now scale x, y (and
z) by a power of two where ρ² (or r²) is below the normal range, so cos φ keeps its precision next
to the z-axis; every other point's bits unchanged (a select by 1).
Helmholtz CH4.9b2 made those directions public as `orthonormal::LeafDirections` (cos θ, sin θ, cos φ,
sin φ per lane, `of(points)`; the private `Lanes` renamed, no value changed), so that the batched
L2P's Cartesian gradient takes the harmonics' own frame; `tests/orthonormal/leaf.rs` holds them to
the reference's `hypot` angles within 4 u (2 u measured) and exactly at the centre, the poles, the
axes and next to the z-axis.

## Rules
- Read docs/CONVENTIONS.md before changing any formula; never change a convention here.
- Generic over T: RealScalar; no allocation in hot paths (write into caller slices).
- No trigonometric functions in harmonic evaluation (CONVENTIONS §3.5).
- Before finishing: `cargo clippy -p tetraleaf-math --all-targets -- -D warnings`
  and `cargo test -p tetraleaf-math` must pass.

## Allowed dependencies
num-traits, num-complex (Helmholtz CH1.2b, signed off 2026-10-30); dev-dependencies:
proptest, approx, serde_json (fixture loading).
Anything else needs a note in the PR.

## Test oracle
Fixtures in tetraleaf-math/fixtures/ (generated by tools/fixtures/), plus the identities in
CONVENTIONS §3.4 and the rotation properties in §3.8. For `orthonormal`,
`fixtures/helmholtz_angular.json` (`tools/fixtures/gen_helmholtz_angular.py`), the
identities of §3.15, and the Laplace harmonics and blocks conjugated by N at p ≤ 30.
For `radial`, `fixtures/helmholtz_radial.json` (`tools/fixtures/gen_helmholtz_radial.py`,
spike S3's sets) and the Wronskians and relations of §3.15. For `coaxial`,
`fixtures/helmholtz_coaxial.json` (`tools/fixtures/gen_helmholtz_coaxial.py`, spike S2's
sets) in S2's FMM-weighted error, the addition theorems pointwise, and independent
methods against each other over whole tables.
